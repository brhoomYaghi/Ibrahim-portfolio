# Ibrahim Yaghi — AI Platform Engineer & Agentic Systems Architect
## Portfolio Website Architecture & Development Blueprint

### 1. Project Overview & Executive Mission
This repository houses the personal portfolio and interactive digital showcase for **Ibrahim Yaghi**, AI Platform Engineer & Agentic Systems Architect at Zain Jordan.
The website delivers an executive-level, Apple-style product storytelling and creative consultancy experience. It highlights 5+ years of production AI engineering, enterprise-scale cost savings (~4M JOD / ~$5.6M USD), an in-house agent orchestration framework with 8 specialized production AI agents, the Botzilla RAG platform, and PySpark pipeline optimization (8 hours to <10 minutes).

### 2. Design System & Aesthetics
- **Palette**:
  - Primary Background: Executive Deep Navy (`#060B18`, `#0A1128`) with high-end Light/Off-White clean variant (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`).
  - Text & Surface: Crystal White (`#FFFFFF`), Off-White (`#F1F5F9`), Slate Silver (`#94A3B8`).
  - Accents: Electric Royal Blue (`#2563EB`, `#3B82F6`), Luminous Cyan/Sky (`#38BDF8`), Subtle Platinum Glass borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Typography**: Apple & Enterprise consultancy typography via Google Fonts (Outfit for headings, Inter for high-legibility body, JetBrains Mono for architecture & telemetry).
- **Tone**: Sophisticated, authoritative, elegant, and corporate-executive. No cyberpunk or neon gamer aesthetics.
